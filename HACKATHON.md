## Prizes
Grand $10,000, runners-up $7,000 and $4,000, then $3,000 each for Best iOS,
Best Android, Best Web/Chrome. Our target is Best Web/Chrome.
About 2,700 people had registered by 17 Nov. A win is not guaranteed.
Winners are announced around 21 Dec; payment follows within 60 days of tax forms
and may be reduced by withholding.

## Judging
Stage 1 is pass/fail: all required items present.
Stage 2: four criteria, equal weight, scored 1 to 5.
1. Technological implementation: build quality and effective use of hybrid inference
2. Design: a complete product, not a proof of concept
3. Potential impact: a real problem for a real audience
4. Quality of the idea: creative, non-obvious use of Firebase AI Logic
Judges may score from the description, images and video alone.

## Submission checklist (stage 1)
- [ ] Platform category selected: Web/Chrome
- [ ] Text description: how Firebase AI Logic is used, features, value proposition
- [ ] Public GitHub repo with all source, assets and setup instructions
- [ ] Open source license visible in the repo About section (Apache-2.0)
- [ ] Repo shows Firebase AI Logic imported and called at runtime
- [ ] No production keys, secrets, debug tokens or personal data anywhere
- [ ] Demo video: public on YouTube, 3 minutes max, English or English subtitles,
      shows the extension running in Chrome with on-device and cloud both visible
- [ ] Video opens with audience, problem and how hybrid helps; hybrid shown early
- [ ] No third-party trademarks or unlicensed content in the video
- [ ] Testing instructions so judges can load the extension (hosted link encouraged)
- [ ] App supports English
- [ ] No commits needed after the deadline (later commits are not evaluated)
